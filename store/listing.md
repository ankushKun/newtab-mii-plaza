# Store listing

What to paste into the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons. Keep it in the voice of the project page: plain, and true to what the extension does.

## Summary

The manifest `description` in `wxt.config.ts`, which Chrome shows under the name (132 characters at most):

```
Every new tab becomes the Wii U's Wara Wara Plaza, with your favourite sites around the edge and Miis hanging out in the middle.
```

## Description

```
New Tab Mii Plaza turns every new tab into the Wii U's Wara Wara Plaza. Your favourite sites float around the edge as blocks, and Miis hang out in the middle.

Your sites
- Pin up to ten sites. Each block takes the site's own icon and colour, or a picture or GIF you choose.
- Click a block to open the site, right-click it to edit it, or drag it to give it a spin.
- Reorder the blocks in Settings, and export or import them as a backup.

Your Miis
- Drop in pictures of Mii QR codes from a 3DS or Wii U and those Miis join the plaza.
- Pick them up and throw them around. They chatter as they go, and you can mute them.

Everything else
- Light, dark or automatic theme, and an optional clock.
- It stops drawing after five minutes without input, and tabs in the background give their graphics memory back.
- No account, no ads and no tracking. Pins sync through your browser's own account, and everything else stays on your computer.

Open Settings with the gear in the bottom right corner, or press ?.

Privacy policy: https://ankush.one/newtab-mii-plaza/privacy/
Support: https://ankush.one/newtab-mii-plaza/support/
Source: https://github.com/ankushKun/newtab-mii-plaza

New Tab Mii Plaza is a fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo. Mii, Wii U and Nintendo 3DS are trademarks of Nintendo.
```

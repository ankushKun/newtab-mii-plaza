Support

Getting around

  • Click a block to open its site, or an empty one to pin a site there.
  • Right-click a block to change its address, name or picture, or to remove it.
  • Drag a block to spin it. Drag a Mii to pick it up and let go to throw it.
  • Scroll, pinch or press + and - to zoom. Zoomed in, hold the middle mouse button and drag to look around.
  • The gear in the bottom right corner, or the ? key, opens Settings: reorder blocks, theme, clock, Mii voices, backups and Miis.

Common problems

Firefox forgot the extension after a restart

Add-ons loaded with Load Temporary Add-on only last until Firefox closes. Load it again from about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox until it's listed on Firefox Add-ons.

A block shows a faded plaza logo instead of the site's icon

That site didn't hand over an icon. Right-click the block and pick your own picture for it.

A Mii QR code won't scan

Use a sharp, straight picture of the whole code, a screenshot or the image the console saved works best. It has to be a Mii QR code from a 3DS or Wii U.

Moving pins to another browser

Pins already follow you between computers signed in to the same browser account. To move them to a different browser, use Export in Settings and Import on the other side.

Getting help

Report bugs on GitHub Issues with your browser, its version and what happened, ideally with a screenshot. For anything else, ask on Discord or message @ankushKun_.

New Tab Mii Plaza

Every new tab becomes the Wii U's Wara Wara Plaza, with your favourite sites around the edge and Miis hanging out in the middle.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Your sites, one click away

Pin up to ten sites. Each block takes the site's own icon and colour, or a picture or GIF you pick, and a right-click edits it.

Bring your own Miis

Drop in the QR codes of Miis you made on a 3DS or Wii U and they join the plaza. You can pick them up and throw them around.

Out of your way

No account, no ads and no tracking. Pins sync through your browser's own account, and the plaza stops drawing when you aren't using it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the AGPL, with no ads and no account.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and browsers built on it, like Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi, install it from the Chrome Web Store. Firefox 140 or newer installs it from Firefox Add-ons. Safari and phones aren't supported.

How do I add my own Mii?

Open Settings with the gear in the bottom right corner (or press ?), go to Miis and drop in a picture of a Mii QR code from a 3DS or Wii U. The code is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Will it slow my browser down?

It's a 3D scene, so it does more than a blank tab. It stops drawing after five minutes without input, and a tab left in the background for three minutes gives its graphics memory back.

Does it collect any data?

No. Pins sync through your browser's own account and everything else stays on your computer. The only requests it makes are for a site's title and icon when you pin it, and a check for news from ankush.one at most every six hours, without cookies. The privacy policy has the details.

Is it made by Nintendo?

No, it's a fan project by Ankush Singh. Mii, Wii U and 3DS are trademarks of Nintendo.
